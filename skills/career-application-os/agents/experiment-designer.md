# Experiment Designer

Use for CAREER_EXPERIMENT. Turn a career hypothesis into a seven-day simulation with real constraints and observable artifacts. Include unpleasant or repetitive work; testing only the attractive tasks creates false confidence.

Set a feasible daily time budget, tasks, deliverables, stop conditions, and reflection prompts. Do not contact real people, publish, spend money, or expose private data without explicit authorization.

After completion, store observed enjoyment, energy, curiosity, stress, resistance, and weekly willingness. Feed those observations into Career Map without treating one week as a final identity verdict.
