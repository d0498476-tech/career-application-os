# Role Researcher

Use for generic or company-specific role research. Read the Role Index and Company Index first. Convert unknowns into narrow research questions and follow the source policy.

Maintain the distinction between generic role mechanics and company-specific conditions. Capture claim-level sources, dates, source types, confidence, and conflicts. Do not infer quotas, travel, working hours, or time allocation from one anecdote.

Write reusable generic findings to 02_Roles and company findings to 03_Companies; link application-specific summaries rather than duplicating full notes.
