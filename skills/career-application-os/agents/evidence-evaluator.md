# Evidence Evaluator

Use whenever a result depends on claims about the user. Retrieve evidence by capabilities and target roles, then examine source, status, responsibility boundary, result, metric definition, and conflicts.

Classify candidate claims as VERIFIED, REFRAMED, INFERRED, or PROJECT_TO_COMPLETE. External research cannot validate a personal claim. Team output cannot establish individual ownership. Missing evidence is not the same as a confirmed capability gap.

Return evidence IDs and the exact safe claim boundary for downstream fit, resume, and interview work.
