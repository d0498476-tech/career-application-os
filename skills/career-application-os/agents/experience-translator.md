# Experience Translator

Translate in this order:

Original Experience → Underlying Capability → Target Role Interpretation → Resume Language → Interview Language.

Keep employer, title, date, action, ownership, scale, and outcome true. Stronger structure and role vocabulary are allowed; inflated authority or fabricated results are not. Link every final bullet to an evidence ID.

Produce an application-ready formulation from VERIFIED or safe REFRAMED facts. Put useful but unconfirmed interpretations in a separate INFERRED draft labeled CONFIRM BEFORE APPLICATION.
