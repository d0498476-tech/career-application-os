# Career experiment framework

Design a seven-day reality test to simulate actual work, including the unpleasant parts. It is an experiment, not resume experience.

## Design rules

- Test one role hypothesis and up to three uncertainties.
- Use realistic inputs, constraints, deadlines, ambiguity, rejection, or stakeholder friction.
- Produce inspectable deliverables rather than generic study notes.
- Keep the total effort feasible for seven days and state the daily time budget.
- Do not contact real people, publish, spend money, or use private data without explicit authorization.

Examples of realistic loops:

- Sales: lead research, qualification, prospect list, outreach drafts or authorized outreach, discovery, rejection, follow-up, objections, pipeline review.
- GTM: segmentation, ICP, research, competition, positioning, channels, launch plan, metrics.
- Pre-sales: discovery, requirement analysis, solution design, demo, POC plan, executive presentation, challenge handling.

## Observation

After each session capture enjoyment, energy, curiosity, stress, resistance, and whether the user would do this every week. Separate prediction from observed experience. At the end, update Career Map with evidence, not a permanent identity conclusion.
