# External source policy

Use multiple source categories when important claims affect career decisions or applications.

## Priority

1. Official job description
2. Official company site and product documentation
3. Similar official openings
4. Employee experience
5. Candidate interview experience
6. Xiaohongshu and 牛客
7. LinkedIn, Reddit, and Glassdoor
8. Employee interviews and industry research

Social posts and career marketing content are leads, not authority. Claims about compensation, easy entry, travel, hiring urgency, rapid growth, unlimited income, culture, or work intensity need corroboration.

## Claim record

For every decision-relevant external claim, record:

- claim
- source title and URL
- publication or observation date when available
- access date
- source_type: OFFICIAL, EMPLOYEE_EXPERIENCE, CANDIDATE_EXPERIENCE, COMMUNITY_CONSENSUS, INFERENCE, or MARKETING_CONTENT
- confidence: HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW
- verification_status: VERIFIED, PARTIALLY_VERIFIED, UNVERIFIED, or CONFLICTING

Separate known facts from candidate interpretation. Cite sources near the claim in generated research notes. Do not invent time-allocation percentages or generalize one anecdote to a whole company.
