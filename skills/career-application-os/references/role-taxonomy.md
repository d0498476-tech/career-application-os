# Role taxonomy

Do not infer work reality from title alone. Classify the role by repeated work, customer, decision rights, deliverables, and KPI.

Common archetypes include Product Manager, Growth PM, GTM, Product Marketing, Pre-sales, Solution Consultant, Solution Sales, Technical Sales, Overseas Sales, Channel Sales, Key Account Sales, SDR, BDR, Account Management, Business Development, Customer Success, and International Business.

For each role, name:

- Primary Archetype: the dominant repeated work and outcome
- Secondary Archetype: the meaningful adjacent responsibility
- Why: JD duties and evidence that support the classification
- Boundary: similar titles or archetypes this role is not, and why

Use the archetype to choose evidence and research; keep the employer's official title unchanged in external documents.
